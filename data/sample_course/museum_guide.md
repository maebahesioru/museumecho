# Cedar City Museum — Visitor Guide 2026
# A fictional museum guide for demonstration. All exhibits are invented.

## WELCOME
Cedar City Museum is a natural history and culture museum with four galleries.
Open Tuesday-Sunday 9:00-17:00 (last entry 16:15). Closed Mondays.
Address: 45 Cedar Avenue, Cedar City. Admission: adults $12, students $6, under 12 free.
Audio guides are free with admission — this app is the audio guide.

## GALLERY 1 — DINOSAUR HALL

### Tyrannosaurus Rex Skeleton ("Rex")
- Location: Gallery 1, center platform
- This adult T. rex is 12 meters long and 4 meters tall, one of the most complete skeletons ever found (about 85% of the bones are real).
- Fun fact: its teeth were replaced throughout life; a single tooth could be up to 30 centimeters long including the root.
- Best photo spot: the front-left corner.

### Triceratops Family Group
- Location: Gallery 1, east wall diorama
- Three Triceratops: an adult and two juveniles, arranged as a family group eating low vegetation.
- Their head frill could grow over 2 meters wide; it likely helped with display and species recognition as much as defense.

### Fossil Dig Table (hands-on)
- Location: Gallery 1, exit area
- Kids can brush sand to uncover replica fossils. Open 10:00-12:00 and 14:00-16:00.
- Museum staff present; no reservation needed.

## GALLERY 2 — ANCIENT EGYPT

### Mummy of the Temple Singer (Nes-Amun)
- Location: Gallery 2, dark room (left)
- A singer from a temple choir, about 3,000 years old. Her coffin is painted with scenes from the Book of the Dead.
- Please keep voices low in this room; flash photography is not allowed.

### Daily Life Case
- Location: Gallery 2, center
- Objects from a worker's house: bread molds, a wooden comb, children's toys, a cat's mummy.
- The cat mummy shows how pets were valued — cats were associated with the goddess Bastet.

## GALLERY 3 — SPACE

### Walk Through the Solar System
- Location: Gallery 3, main corridor
- A scale walk from the Sun to Neptune. Each step is roughly 3 million km.
- The corridor ends at "Pluto Corner", explaining the 2006 reclassification debate.

### Meteorite Touch Sample
- Location: Gallery 3, end of corridor
- A 4.5-billion-year-old iron meteorite you may touch. It is part of the oldest material on Earth.

## GALLERY 4 — ART AND CULTURE

### Cedar City Gallery: Local Artists 2026
- Location: Gallery 4, west wing
- Works by 14 local artists responding to the theme "Rivers". The exhibition changes each summer.

### Hands-On Studio
- Location: Gallery 4, east wing
- Drop-in art activities daily 10:00-16:00. Materials included with admission.

## FACILITIES AND PRACTICAL INFO

### Café Cedar
- Location: Ground floor, near the main entrance
- Hours: 9:30-16:30. Sandwiches, soups, kids' menu. Vegetarian and gluten-free options.
- Outside food may be eaten in the Garden Court, not in the galleries.

### Museum Shop
- Location: Ground floor, by the ticket desk
- Open same hours as the museum. Dinosaur plush toys, postcards, books for all ages.

### Accessibility
- Wheelchairs are available for loan at the ticket desk (no charge, first-come).
- Elevators connect all galleries. Dinosaur Hall has a ramp to the platform.
- A quiet room (sensory-friendly) is located next to Gallery 2.
- Guide dogs and assistance dogs are welcome.

### Tours and Programs
- Free guided highlight tours: 11:00 and 15:00 daily (English), 13:30 (Spanish).
- Story time for children (ages 3-7): Saturday 10:30 in the Garden Court.
- Evening lecture series: first Friday of each month, 18:30. Check the website for topics.

### Suggested Visit Routes
- 30 minutes: Dinosaur Hall → Meteorite Touch Sample → exit through the shop.
- 2 hours: Dinosaur Hall → Ancient Egypt → Space → café break → Art and Culture.
- With young children: Fossil Dig Table → cat mummy → hands-on studio → story time (Saturdays).
